# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Israeli couples planning their wedding. They arrive emotionally invested — this is one of the most significant purchases of their lives. They're shopping on mobile while distracted, comparing photographers, and looking for someone they can trust to capture moments they can't get back. Decision window is short; emotional resonance wins before features do.

## Product Purpose

A premium one-page portfolio for Michael Lurie — Israeli wedding photographer and videographer since 1992, 5,000+ events. The site must position him at the top of the Israeli wedding market. It replaces an underperforming WordPress/Elementor site. Success looks like a qualified couple sending a WhatsApp message within seconds of landing.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic. Unhurried. Personal.

The voice is warm and direct — Michael speaking to the couple, not a studio broadcasting to leads. The aesthetic is high-end editorial: dark, luxurious, image-first. Think wedding film house, not photography studio widget.

## Anti-references

- Generic Elementor / Wix wedding photography templates
- White-background, card-grid portfolio layouts
- Colorful or playful wedding sites (pastels, botanical, boho)
- Sites that feel like a brochure rather than a filmmaker's reel
- Any UI that competes visually with the photography

## Design Principles

1. **The image is the product** — every UI decision either serves the photography or gets out of the way
2. **Sell the feeling, not the feature list** — emotion before credentials; trust before specs
3. **One clear door** — every section funnels toward the WhatsApp CTA; nothing competes with it
4. **Dark restraint** — the luxury is in what's not there: no clutter, no noise, no decoration for its own sake
5. **RTL as a first-class citizen** — the Hebrew reading experience is not an afterthought; it defines the layout logic

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Hebrew RTL throughout. Autoplay video must be muted and have `playsinline` for iOS. All animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. WhatsApp link must be functional on mobile (primary conversion channel).
